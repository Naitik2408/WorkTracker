# Personal Life Dashboard --- My Complete Idea

## 1. The Core Idea

I want a personal dashboard that helps me manage my life like the CEO of
my own life. It should bring the important areas of my life into one
clear place so I can understand what is happening, what is progressing,
what is falling behind, and what deserves my attention next.

I do not want a dashboard that is only a collection of numbers or
charts. I want a meaningful picture of my current life, help observing
patterns, and better decisions about how I spend my time and energy. It
should become the place I open every morning to understand my situation
and decide what to focus on today.

## 2. What I Want It to Help Me Do

-   See important areas of my life together instead of checking many
    separate places.
-   Understand what I am currently working on.
-   Track goals, priorities, commitments, and progress.
-   Monitor my career and job-hunting activities.
-   Keep track of personal projects and business ventures.
-   Observe learning and skill-development progress.
-   Monitor health, fitness, finances, and personal responsibilities.
-   Understand where I spend my time and whether it helps me move
    forward.
-   Identify neglected areas, delays, repeated problems, and
    opportunities to improve.
-   Decide what matters most today, this week, and this month.
-   Review progress honestly instead of relying only on memory or
    feelings.

## 3. Main Dashboard --- My Life at a Glance

The main screen should give me a clear overview of my current situation.
I want to understand:

-   What are my most important priorities right now?
-   What are the three most important things I should focus on today?
-   Which projects or responsibilities are active?
-   How is my job search progressing?
-   Am I moving forward with my learning goals?
-   How are my health and fitness habits going?
-   Are there pending tasks, follow-ups, or deadlines?
-   Which areas need more attention?
-   What has improved recently, and what has become worse?

The main dashboard should show useful summaries, not overwhelm me with
every detail. I should be able to open an individual section whenever I
want to explore it further.

## 4. The Seven Main Areas of My Life

### 4.1 Career & Income

This section should help me understand and manage my career direction,
professional growth, and income-related opportunities.

I want to see: - My current career goals and direction. - My job-search
progress. - Companies and roles I am interested in. - Applications I
have submitted and their current status. - Recruiter responses and
interview updates. - Follow-ups I need to make. - Upcoming interviews,
assessments, or career commitments. - Progress toward getting an
internship or job. - Career milestones and achievements.

#### Job-Hunting Experience

Job hunting is important, but I do not want this to become a complicated
application-management tool. My preferred experience is approximately
**95% viewing and monitoring, and 5% taking small actions**.

I mainly want to see: - Which jobs I have applied for. - Whether
applications are pending, shortlisted, rejected, or progressing. - Which
recruiters have replied. - Useful previews or snippets of recruiter
messages. - Which applications need follow-up. - When I last contacted a
recruiter or company. - Links to the relevant email or application.

I want visibility into relevant job-hunting activity, including activity
handled with Gemini and Gmail. Gemini can research opportunities,
shortlist roles, personalize my resume or emails, and prepare
application material. I want to review and approve important external
actions---especially sending emails or submitting applications---before
they happen.

The dashboard should help me understand outcomes rather than make me
manually manage every small detail.

### 4.2 Business & Ventures

This section should give me an overview of the businesses, ventures, and
entrepreneurial ideas I am working on.

I want to see: - My active businesses and ventures. - The current status
and purpose of each. - Important ongoing activities. - Pending work and
next steps. - Progress toward milestones. - Business-related
opportunities and decisions. - Which ventures are receiving attention
and which are being neglected.

For example, Klarone is one venture I have worked on. I want to see its
progress without mixing it up with my career or personal
responsibilities.

### 4.3 Learning & Skills

This section should help me manage my learning journey and understand
whether I am actually improving.

I want to track: - Skills I am currently learning. - Learning goals. -
Topics completed, in progress, and still to learn. - Practice activities
and related projects. - Revision and consistency. - Learning
milestones. - Areas where I am struggling or need more practice.

I do not want to measure learning only by hours spent. I also want to
understand what I can actually do now compared with what I could do
earlier, and whether my learning supports my career goals.

### 4.4 Health & Fitness

This section should help me maintain awareness of my physical health,
fitness activities, and consistency.

I want to see: - Fitness goals. - Workout activity and consistency. -
General activity and movement. - Important health-related habits. -
Progress toward fitness goals. - Changes over time. - Areas where I have
been consistent or inconsistent.

The purpose is to observe progress and build consistency, not to turn
the dashboard into a medical system.

### 4.5 Finance

This section should help me understand my personal financial situation.

I want to see: - Income and expenses. - Where my money is going. -
Savings and financial goals. - Important upcoming payments. - Changes in
spending patterns. - Progress toward financial goals.

I want to understand my financial position at a glance and explore
details when necessary.

### 4.6 Personal Operations

This section should help me manage practical responsibilities that keep
my life organized.

I want to see: - Important personal tasks. - Pending responsibilities. -
Appointments and commitments. - Reminders and deadlines. - Things I need
to arrange or take care of. - Recurring responsibilities. - Important
personal information or references I need to access.

I want to avoid forgetting important things because they are spread
across different apps, notes, or conversations.

### 4.7 Personal Growth

This section should help me reflect on myself and understand my overall
personal development.

I want to observe: - Personal goals and milestones. - Habits and
consistency. - Personal achievements. - Areas where I want to improve. -
Reflections and observations. - Patterns in my behavior and progress. -
Whether daily actions align with my longer-term direction.

The purpose is to understand myself better, not to judge myself through
scores.

## 5. Daily Dashboard --- What I Want to See Every Morning

The daily view is one of the most important parts of the experience.
When I open the dashboard in the morning, I want:

-   Today's date and a useful daily overview.
-   My top three priorities.
-   Important tasks and commitments.
-   Upcoming deadlines or follow-ups.
-   Active project work.
-   Job-hunting actions that need attention.
-   Learning goals or practice planned for the day.
-   Health and fitness activities.
-   Important reminders.
-   Areas needing attention because something is pending or delayed.

I want to understand the day quickly and decide what to work on first.
The dashboard should help me prioritize rather than simply display a
long list of tasks.

## 6. Goals, Milestones & Long-Term Progress

I want to manage goals at different levels: long-term, monthly, weekly,
and daily.

For each important goal, I want to understand: - What I am trying to
achieve and why it matters. - What progress I have made. - What
milestones remain. - What I should do next. - Whether I am on track,
delayed, or need to reconsider the goal.

I want to see how smaller daily and weekly actions contribute to larger
goals. I should also be able to review or change priorities when
circumstances change.

## 7. Projects & Activities Overview

I want a central view of the projects and meaningful activities I am
currently working on.

For each project, I want to see: - Project name and purpose. - Current
status and stage. - Important milestones. - Recent progress. - Pending
decisions or work. - Next meaningful action. - Whether it needs my
attention.

I may have personal projects, learning projects, portfolio projects, and
business-related projects. I want to see them together without treating
all of them as if they have the same purpose or importance.

### How I Want to Work With Projects

I want Gemini to help me understand my current projects and decide what
deserves attention. When I have a new idea, I want to discuss and
evaluate it before it becomes an active project. I do not want every
passing idea to automatically become a project.

Once a project is sufficiently clear and I decide to proceed, it should
become part of my active project overview.

For new projects, I want a structured journey covering research,
validation, planning, design, implementation, testing, launch, and
improvement as appropriate. For existing projects, I want to understand
their current condition and continue from where they actually stand
instead of unnecessarily starting over.

## 8. Analytics, Charts & Observations

I do not want charts merely for decoration. I want analytics that help
me understand my life and make better decisions.

I want daily, weekly, monthly, and longer-term views where useful.
Examples of observations:

-   How consistently am I working toward career goals?
-   How is job-search activity changing over time?
-   How many applications are progressing, pending, or receiving
    responses?
-   Am I consistently learning, or do I keep starting and stopping?
-   Which skills are improving?
-   How much progress am I making on projects?
-   Am I maintaining my fitness routine?
-   Where is my money going?
-   Which areas receive most of my attention?
-   Which areas are repeatedly neglected?
-   Are my priorities reflected in how I actually spend my time?
-   What has improved compared with the previous week or month?

I want useful comparisons, trends, summaries, and visualizations that
make these patterns understandable.

### Multiple Ways to Observe the Same Information

For example, job-hunting information could appear as: - A list of
applications. - Applications grouped by status. - A timeline of
application activity. - A summary of responses and follow-ups. - A
comparison of activity across weeks or months.

Project progress, learning consistency, fitness activity, and finances
should also have visual views that make sense for each area.

The purpose of analytics is to help me discover patterns and weaknesses
I might otherwise miss.

## 9. Daily, Weekly & Monthly Reviews

### Daily Review

At the end of the day, I want to understand: - What did I plan to do? -
What did I actually complete? - What remains pending? - What progress
did I make? - What interrupted or delayed me? - What should I carry
forward to tomorrow?

### Weekly Review

At the end of each week, I want to understand: - Important
achievements. - Which goals moved forward. - Job-search progress. - What
I learned. - Project progress. - Health and fitness consistency. - What
I neglected. - Priorities for next week.

### Monthly Review

At the end of each month, I want a broader view: - What changed in my
life? - Which goals progressed? - Which milestones did I achieve? - What
did I learn about myself? - What patterns can I observe? - Where did I
spend most of my time and energy? - What should I continue, stop, or
change?

These reviews should help me make decisions instead of merely producing
reports.

## 10. Gemini as My Personal Advisor

I want Gemini to be more than a tool that displays information. I want
it to act as a personal advisor that helps me understand my situation
and decide what to do next.

I want to be able to ask: - What should I focus on today? - Which
current project needs attention? - Am I making enough progress toward my
career goals? - What should I follow up on in my job search? - Which
learning activity should I prioritize? - What have I been neglecting
recently? - What changed compared with last week? - Why does it look
like I am falling behind on a particular goal? - What should I improve
next month?

Recommendations should be based on my actual activities, goals,
progress, and current situation---not generic motivational advice.
Gemini should explain why it recommends something and help me make
informed decisions.

Gemini may research opportunities, prepare personalized emails, organize
information, and prepare other useful actions. For important external
actions, such as sending emails or submitting applications, I want to
review and approve them first. I want to remain in control of decisions
that affect my career, money, commitments, or communication with other
people.

## 11. Information From Different Apps and Tools

My activities and information may exist across different applications
and services. I want the dashboard to provide a central view of relevant
information from those places.

Examples: - Job-related emails and recruiter replies. - Job applications
and their current status. - Information about ongoing projects. -
Learning activities. - Health and fitness information. - Financial
information. - Calendar events and important commitments.

I do not want to repeatedly visit multiple applications just to
understand my overall situation.

For example, in the job-hunting section, I mainly want to see relevant
application and email information and open Gmail when I need to read or
respond in detail. The dashboard should show the bigger picture while
allowing me to visit the original application whenever I need more
detail or want to take an action.

## 12. Priorities and Decision-Making

One of the biggest reasons I want this dashboard is to reduce confusion
about what I should work on.

I may have multiple active projects, career responsibilities, learning
goals, and personal commitments.

I want the dashboard to help me answer: - What is most important right
now? - What is urgent? - What can wait? - Which activity contributes
most to my current goals? - Am I taking on too many things at once? -
Which project should receive my attention? - What should I stop doing or
postpone?

I want priorities to be visible and help me make realistic decisions
about my time and energy.

## 13. Reminders and Important Notifications

I want to be aware of things that require my attention, such as: - Job
application follow-ups. - Recruiter responses. - Interview dates. -
Project deadlines. - Important personal commitments. - Upcoming
payments. - Goal milestones. - Tasks that have remained pending for too
long.

Reminders should be useful and relevant rather than constant
notifications that create more distraction.

## 14. The Overall User Experience I Want

The dashboard should feel like a personal control center for my life.

I want it to be: - Clear and easy to understand. - Organized into
meaningful life areas. - Focused on useful information rather than
unnecessary details. - Easy to navigate between the overall view and
individual sections. - Useful every morning and during weekly or monthly
reviews. - Helpful for quick decisions as well as deeper analysis. -
Flexible enough to reflect changes in my goals and responsibilities.

I do not want to spend more time managing the dashboard than managing my
actual life.

## 15. What Success Looks Like to Me

The dashboard will be successful if:

1.  I can open one place and understand the overall condition of my
    life.
2.  I can clearly see my current priorities and what I should focus on
    today.
3.  I can monitor my career and job search without manually checking
    many different places.
4.  I can understand the progress of my projects and ventures.
5.  I can track my learning and see whether I am actually improving.
6.  I can observe my health, fitness, and financial habits.
7.  I can identify areas that I have been neglecting.
8.  I can compare my progress over time and discover useful patterns.
9.  Gemini can help me interpret my information and make better
    decisions.
10. I can review my day, week, and month with a clear understanding of
    what happened.
11. I feel more organized, intentional, and in control of my
    responsibilities.

## 16. The Vision

Ultimately, I want to build a **Personal Life Operating Dashboard**---a
central place where I can see, understand, and manage the important
parts of my life.

I want to approach my life like the CEO of my own life: understand the
current situation, set priorities, monitor progress, make decisions, and
improve continuously.

The dashboard should not decide my life for me. It should give me the
clarity and information I need to make better decisions myself.

> I want one place that helps me understand where I stand in life, what
> I am working toward, what needs my attention, and whether my daily
> actions are moving me in the right direction.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Scope Note

This document describes the idea from my point of view as the person who
will use the dashboard. It intentionally focuses on needs, expectations,
and user experience.

It does not define the technology stack, database, APIs, system
architecture, technical implementation, or detailed engineering
requirements. Those should be developed separately after this idea has
been reviewed and understood.
